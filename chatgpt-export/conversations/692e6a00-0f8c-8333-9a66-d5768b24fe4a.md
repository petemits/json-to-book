Yes — **as the OWNER, you *can* remove roommates**, but **how you remove them depends on whether they are *tenants* or *guests***.  
Because you are in **Ontario (Mississauga)**, the **Residential Tenancies Act (RTA)** applies.

Below is a clear explanation so you don’t get in trouble legally.

---

# ✅ **1. Are your roommates *tenants* or *guests*?**
This determines your rights.

### **A. If they pay rent, have a room, or contribute to bills**  
➡️ They are **tenants or licensees**.  
➡️ **You CANNOT just kick them out** (illegal eviction).  
➡️ You must use proper eviction forms.

### **B. If they are staying for free, no agreement, no rent**  
➡️ They are **guests**.  
➡️ You *can* tell them to leave **without the Landlord and Tenant Board**.  
➡️ But you still must give **reasonable notice** (usually 24–72 hours).

---

# ✅ **2. If they are tenants, here is the legal way to remove them**
Even if you are the owner **living in the home**, the law still applies.

### ✔️ Step 1 — Give the correct eviction notice  
Depending on the reason:

| Reason | Correct Form |
|-------|--------------|
| You want them to leave because *you* need the space | **N11 (mutual agreement)** or **N12** |
| They are causing problems, damage, noise, threats | **N5** |
| They are not paying their share | **N4** |
| They are overcrowding or breaking rules | **N7** |

### ✔️ Step 2 — If they don’t leave, you apply to the LTB  
You file an **application to the Landlord and Tenant Board (LTB)**.  
**You cannot physically remove them yourself.**

### ✔️ Step 3 — Sheriff removes them  
If the board approves your eviction, only the **Sheriff** can remove them.

---

# ✅ **3. If they are guests (not tenants)**  
If your roommates **never paid rent, never signed anything**, and are basically staying in your home:

✔️ You can ask them to leave  
✔️ You can give written notice (ex: 24–48 hours)  
✔️ Police can help if they refuse and they are trespassing

---

# ⚠️ **4. IMPORTANT: You cannot change locks or remove their belongings**  
Ontario law considers that **illegal eviction**, even if you own the property.

If you change locks or remove their things, they can file:  
✔️ Police report  
✔️ LTB complaint  
✔️ Civil claim

---

# ✅ **5. Your rights as the owner**
As an owner living on the property:

- You can select who lives with you  
- You can end shared living arrangements  
- You can file legal eviction notices  
- You can remove guests with reasonable notice  
- You can’t violate the Residential Tenancies Act  
- You cannot use force or threats
