## 📌 2️⃣ The Condo Corporation Can’t Retroactively Restrict Ownership

The Act *does not give the board authority to seize, retain, or block an owner from removing their own property* inside their unit. > **Section 18(1)**  
> “The corporation may own, acquire, encumber and dispose of real and personal property **only for purposes that are consistent with the objects and duties of the corporation**.” citeturn0search5

👉 That confirms the condo corporation can’t control property *it doesn’t own* for reasons outside its legal duties.

## 📌 3️⃣ Reasonableness and “No Dangerous Conditions” Rules

The Act does say:

> **Section 117**  
> “No person shall permit a condition to exist or carry on an activity in a unit… if the condition or the activity is likely to **damage the property** or cause **injury or illness**.” citeturn0search9

That means:

- The board **can require remediation** if your unit poses a risk to others (like severe infestation).
