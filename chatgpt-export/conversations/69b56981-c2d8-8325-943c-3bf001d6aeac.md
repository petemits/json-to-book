## **1. Concept: Clay Biomass Gasifier**

A **biomass gasifier** converts solid biomass (wood, crop residue, millet husks, rice husks) into combustible gas (“syngas”) using **low-oxygen pyrolysis**.  

- Fuel is heated in a **small oxygen-limited chamber**.  
- Gas rises to a **combustion chamber** where it burns cleanly.  
- Heat is used for cooking, and with a **small generator or thermoelectric module**, can produce electricity.  

**Clay/earthen materials are perfect because:**

- They resist heat  
- Act as insulation → reduces fuel loss  
- Can be shaped easily into cylinders, boxes, or domes  
- Very cheap (₹50–₹200 for a small stove)
