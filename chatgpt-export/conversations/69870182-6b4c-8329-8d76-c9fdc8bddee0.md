### 2. Can they make you pay for treatment of *other* units?
**Not automatically.** This is where limits apply.

Most condos follow this structure:

#### ✅ You usually pay for:
- Treatment **inside your own unit**, if:
  - The infestation is found there
  - Or you failed to report it promptly

#### ❌ You usually do NOT pay for:
- Neighbouring units
- Units above/below
- Hallways or common elements

- The condo declaration/by-laws **explicitly say costs can be charged back**
- There is **clear evidence** the infestation originated from your unit
- You ignored warnings or delayed treatment and caused spread

Dead bugs alone are **not strong proof** of origin.

Typically:
- **Condo corporation pays first** (as a common expense)
- They may later try to recover costs **if fault is proven**

Many condos treat surrounding units as **preventive containment**, not owner fault.
