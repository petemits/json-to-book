In Ontario, the procedure for filing a **civil lawsuit** depends on the type of claim, the amount of money involved, and the nature of the dispute. I’ll break it down for you:  

---

### 1. **Decide the Court**
- **Small Claims Court** → for claims **up to $35,000** (e.g., contracts, property damage, unpaid bills).  
- **Ontario Superior Court of Justice** → for claims **over $35,000** or more complex cases (e.g., malpractice, negligence, personal injury, constitutional issues).  

---

### 2. **Prepare Your Claim**
- **Small Claims Court**  
  - Complete **Form 7A: Plaintiff’s Claim**.  
  - Available online here: [Plaintiff’s Claim (Form 7A)](https://www.ontariocourtforms.on.ca/en/small-claims-court-forms/).  
  - You’ll need to outline: names of parties, what happened, amount claimed, and supporting documents.  

- **Superior Court of Justice**  
  - You usually start with a **Statement of Claim (Form 14A or 14B)**.  
  - Forms are available here: [Superior Court Civil Forms](https://www.ontariocourtforms.on.ca/en/rules-of-civil-procedure-forms/).  
  - A lawyer is strongly advised for these cases since the process is more complex.  

---

### 3. **File the Claim**
- **Online filing**:  
  - [Small Claims Court e-Filing Service](https://www.ontario.ca/page/file-small-claims-court-documents-online) (for claims up to $35,000).  
  - [Civil Claims Online Portal](https://www.ontario.ca/page/file-civil-claim-online) (for Superior Court).  

- **In person**:  
  - At the courthouse where the defendant lives, works, or where the issue happened.  

- **Filing Fee**:  
  - Small Claims → around **$102** to file a Plaintiff’s Claim (extra fees apply as case progresses).  
  - Superior Court → higher fees (e.g., **$229** to issue a Statement of Claim).  

---

### 4. **Serve the Defendant**
- After filing, you must **serve the documents** (personally or via alternatives) within the required time.  
- Proof of service must also be filed with the court.  

---

### 5. **Wait for a Response**
- Defendant has:  
  - **20 days** (in Ontario)  
  - **40 days** (outside Canada/US)  
  - to file a **Statement of Defence**.  

If no defence is filed, you may request a **default judgment**.
