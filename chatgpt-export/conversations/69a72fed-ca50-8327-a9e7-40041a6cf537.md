## 1️⃣ Student Visa (Study Permit)

To study in Canada, you need a **Study Permit**.

### Basic Requirements:
- Acceptance letter from a **Designated Learning Institution (DLI)**
- Proof of funds (tuition + living expenses)
- Clean criminal record (police certificate)
- Medical exam (if required)
- Proof you will leave Canada after studies (unless applying for PR later)

### Process:
1. Get accepted by a DLI  
2. Apply online through **entity["organization","Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada","canadian federal department"] (IRCC)**  
3. Submit biometrics  
4. Wait for approval  
5. Receive Port of Entry Letter  

### After Graduation:
You may qualify for:
- **Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP)**
- Pathway to Permanent Residency (Express Entry)

---

## 2️⃣ Work Visa (Work Permit)

There are two main types:

### A) Employer-Specific Work Permit
- Requires job offer
- Employer may need LMIA (Labour Market Impact Assessment)

### B) Open Work Permit
- No specific employer needed
- Available to:
  - Spouses of students/workers
  - PGWP holders
  - Some humanitarian cases

### Process:
1. Get job offer (if required)
2. Employer applies for LMIA (if required)
3. Apply online through IRCC
4. Biometrics + medical (if required)

---

## 3️⃣ Permanent Residency (PR)

### Most Common Pathway: Express Entry
Managed by IRCC.

Programs under Express Entry:
- Federal Skilled Worker Program
- Canadian Experience Class
- Federal Skilled Trades Program

### How It Works:
1. Create Express Entry profile
2. Get CRS (Comprehensive Ranking System) score
3. Receive Invitation to Apply (ITA)
4. Submit documents
5. Get PR decision

Processing: Usually 6–12 months (varies).
