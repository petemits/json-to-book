# Motion in Condo AGM
*2026-03-27*

**User:**

When condo board meeting AGM what does motion means

---

**Assistant:**

In a condo board **AGM (Annual General Meeting)**, a **motion** is simply a **formal proposal** that members are asked to consider and vote on.

### Think of it like this:
A motion = *“I suggest we do this — do we all agree?”*

---

### 🔹 Example of a motion
- “I move that the condo fees increase by 5%.”
- “I move that we approve the new security contract.”

---

### 🔹 How a motion works step-by-step
1. **Someone proposes it** (called “moving” the motion)  
2. **Another person seconds it** (shows at least two people think it’s worth discussing)  
3. **Discussion happens** (owners can speak for or against it)  
4. **Vote is taken**  
5. **Result** → Passed or defeated  

---

### 🔹 Why motions matter
Motions are how **decisions become official** at the AGM. Without a motion, nothing can be formally approved.

---

### 🔹 Types of motions you might see
- Approving budget or financial statements  
- Electing board members  
- Changing bylaws or rules  
- Hiring management or security companies
