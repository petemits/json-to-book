## 🔹 2) DMX LIGHT SYSTEMS (Computer-Controlled Visual Effects)

DMX systems are the **industry standard for controlling lights, lasers, fog, and effects** in concerts, air shows, and outdoor events.

### 🔧 What DMX Is
- **DMX512** is a digital control protocol
- One computer can control **hundreds of lighting devices**
- No screen needed — visuals appear in the **air or sky**

### 💡 What You Can Control
- Laser beams
- Moving spotlights
- LED bars and panels
- Fog / haze machines (to make beams visible)
- Strobes, color changes, motion patterns

### 🖥️ How It Integrates With a Computer
1. **Computer → USB-DMX interface**
2. Interface → DMX cable
3. DMX cable → lighting devices (daisy-chained)

Common interfaces:
- USB-DMX dongles
- Ethernet-to-DMX (Art-Net / sACN)

### 🎛️ Control Software (Runs on PC)
- Light sequencing
- Real-time control
- Audio-sync
- Timeline automation

Examples:
- Lightjams
- QLC+
- MadMapper
- Resolume Arena (with DMX plugin)

- Light beams become visible in air using **fog/haze**

### ✈️ Air-Show Use Cases

- Timed effects triggered by sound or GPS cues

⚠️ **Important:** Laser DMX systems must follow aviation and eye-safety regulations.
