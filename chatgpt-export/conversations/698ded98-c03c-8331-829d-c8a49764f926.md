If you’re asking about **devices used to detect illicit drugs**, there are several types used by law enforcement, border services, workplaces, and medical settings.

Here’s a clear breakdown:

---

## 1️⃣ Portable Drug Testing Kits (Field Test Kits)
- Small chemical kits
- Substance is placed in a vial → color change indicates possible drug type
- Used by police for quick roadside or field identification  
- **Limitation:** Presumptive only (not 100% accurate)

---

## 2️⃣ Drug Testing Devices (Biological Testing)

These test a person rather than a substance.

### 🔹 Urine Test Kits
- Most common workplace test
- Detects drugs like cannabis, cocaine, opioids, amphetamines
- Detection window: 1–7 days (varies by drug)

### 🔹 Saliva (Oral Fluid) Test
- Used for roadside drug testing
- Detects recent use (usually 6–48 hours)

### 🔹 Hair Testing
- Detects drug use up to 90 days
- Used in legal or employment cases

### 🔹 Blood Testing
- Most accurate
- Used in hospitals or serious legal cases

---

## 3️⃣ Electronic Drug Detection Devices

### 🔹 Ion Mobility Spectrometry (IMS)
- Detects trace drug particles
- Used at airports and border crossings

### 🔹 Handheld Spectrometers (Raman / FTIR)
- Non-contact testing through plastic bags
- Used by law enforcement

---

## 4️⃣ Drug Detection Sensors / Emerging Tech
- Portable AI-based analyzers
- Smartphone-connected test strips
- Wastewater drug monitoring systems (public health)

If you mean **detecting illicit drugs hidden behind walls**, there’s no simple consumer device that can just “scan” a wall and show drugs inside.

Here’s how it realistically works:

---

## 🚫 What Does *Not* Exist
- ❌ No handheld scanner that can see drugs through drywall like X-ray vision  
- ❌ No phone app that can detect drugs inside walls  
- ❌ Standard stud finders cannot detect drugs  

---

## 🔎 What Law Enforcement Uses (High-Level Overview)

These are specialized tools and usually require legal authority:

### 1️⃣ Drug Detection Dogs
- Most effective method
- Can detect odor through walls, vents, and containers

### 2️⃣ Ion Mobility Spectrometry (Trace Detection)
- Detects microscopic drug particles in air or on surfaces
- Usually requires access to the space

### 3️⃣ Thermal Imaging
- Sometimes used if grow operations (like cannabis grows) produce heat
- Does NOT directly detect drugs — only heat anomalies

### 4️⃣ Ground-Penetrating Radar (GPR)
- Can detect hidden compartments
- Cannot identify drugs specifically — only density differences

### 5️⃣ Fiber-Optic Inspection Cameras
- Requires drilling a small hole
- Used when there is probable cause

---

## ⚠️ Important Reality

In residential settings:
- Hidden drugs behind walls are usually discovered due to:
  - Smell
  - Tip-offs
  - Structural modifications
  - Utility anomalies
  - K9 alerts

There is no legal over-the-counter “drug wall detector.”
