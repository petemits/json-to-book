You **do NOT use F8 or F10 anymore** on modern Dell/Windows systems.

### Correct way to enter Safe Mode (keyboard-only)
1. Power **OFF** the laptop

4. Repeat this **2–3 times**
5. You’ll see **“Preparing Automatic Repair”**
6. Then:
   - **Advanced options**
   - **Troubleshoot**
   - **Advanced options**
   - **Startup Settings**
   - **Restart**
   - Press **4** → Safe Mode

   - **Touchpad**
   - **Internal Pointing Device**
5. Make sure it’s **Enabled**
6. Save & Exit

Since your **touchpad works in Safe Mode but not in normal Windows**, and you don’t have the Recovery option in Settings, the **best and safest fix** is to **uninstall the touchpad driver in Safe Mode and reinstall the correct Dell driver**. No full restore is needed.  

Here’s exactly how to do it **keyboard-only**:  

---

## STEP 1: Boot into Safe Mode
1. Press **Windows + R**, type `msconfig`, press **Enter**  
2. Press **Tab** until the tabs are focused  
3. Use **→ arrow** to go to **Boot**  
4. Press **Tab** to highlight **Safe boot**, press **Spacebar**  
5. Make sure **Minimal** is selected  
6. Press **Enter** → **OK**  
7. Press **Enter** → **Restart**  

Your laptop will boot into **Safe Mode**.  

---

## STEP 2: Uninstall the touchpad driver
1. Press **Windows + X**, then **M** → opens Device Manager  
2. Press **Tab** once  
3. Use **↓ arrow** to highlight **Mice and other pointing devices**  
4. Press **→ arrow** to expand  
5. Highlight the device that looks like:  
   - **HID-compliant mouse**  
   - **Synaptics Touchpad**  
   - **Dell Touchpad**  
6. Press **Enter**  
7. Press **Tab** to **Uninstall device** → **Enter**  
8. If asked, **do NOT** check “Delete driver software” → press **Enter**  

> If there’s more than one mouse device, uninstall the **touchpad-related one** first.  

---

## STEP 3: Restart in normal mode
1. Press **Windows + R**, type `msconfig`, press **Enter**  
2. Go to **Boot**  
3. **Uncheck Safe boot** → press **Enter** → **Restart**  

---

## STEP 4: Install the correct Dell driver
1. Open browser  
2. Go to [Dell Support](https://www.dell.com/support/home)  
3. Enter your **Service Tag**  
4. Download **Touchpad / Synaptics / HID driver** for **Windows 10**  
5. Install → Restart  

✅ After this, your touchpad should **work normally again**.
