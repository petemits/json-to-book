## Method 1: Simple Homemade Fruit Juice Powder (Best & Natural)

### You’ll need
- Fresh fruit (orange, mango, strawberry, apple, etc.)
- Blender
- Oven **or** food dehydrator
- Grinder / blender
- Fine sieve
- Airtight container

### Steps
1. **Wash & cut fruit**  
   Remove seeds and peels if needed.

2. **Blend into juice or puree**  
   Add a little water only if required.

3. **Strain (optional)**  
   For clearer juice powder, strain out pulp.

4. **Dry the juice**
   - **Oven method:**  
     - Line a tray with parchment paper  
     - Spread juice thinly  
     - Dry at **60–70°C (140–160°F)** for several hours  
   - **Dehydrator:**  
     - Dry until completely brittle

5. **Grind into powder**  
   Break dried sheets and grind finely.

6. **Sieve & store**  
   Store in an airtight jar away from moisture.

7. **Use**  
   Mix **1–2 tsp powder + water** 🍹

---

## Method 2: Quick Powder Using Juice + Carrier (Easier)

This prevents clumping.

### Extra ingredient
- **Maltodextrin OR cornstarch (very small amount)**

### Steps
1. Mix **fresh juice + 1–2 tsp maltodextrin**
2. Dry as above
3. Grind → free-flowing powder

---

## Method 3: Instant Flavor Powder (Not Natural Juice)

- Sugar
- Citric acid
- Fruit flavor powder
- Food coloring (optional)

👉 This is how **store-bought powdered juice** is made, but it’s not real juice.

---

## Tips
- Must be **completely dry** or it will spoil
- Add **vitamin C powder** for longer shelf life
- Keep away from humidity
