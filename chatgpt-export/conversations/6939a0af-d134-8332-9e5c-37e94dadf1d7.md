Yes — **you *can* pursue a case involving a property manager through Legal Aid Ontario (LAO)**, **but only in certain situations**. It depends on **what the dispute is about** and **your income/financial eligibility**.

Below is a clear breakdown so you know exactly where you stand:

---

# ✅ **When Legal Aid *Can* Help With a Property Manager Case**
LAO may assist if your legal issue involves **housing rights**, especially when your property manager’s actions affect your safety, living conditions, or tenancy status.

You may qualify for help **IF your case fits one of these categories:**

### **1. Eviction or Threat of Eviction**
Legal Aid may help if:
- You are being **evicted improperly**
- Your property manager gave **invalid notices**
- Your landlord/property manager is trying to **force you out**
- You need to defend yourself at the **Landlord and Tenant Board (LTB)**

### **2. Harassment or Illegal Actions by Property Manager**
Examples:
- Entering your unit without permission
- Shutting off utilities
- Threatening or intimidating you
- Not following proper legal notices

### **3. Repairs and Maintenance Disputes Affecting Health/Safety**
LAO may help if:
- Serious repairs are ignored
- Your unit is unsafe or uninhabitable
- Management refuses to fix damage (even if caused by a break-in, etc.)

### **4. Human Rights Issues**
Legal Aid may help if:
- The property manager discriminates based on disability  
(Important: You mentioned schizophrenia — this *can* place you under human rights protection.)
- They refuse reasonable accommodation

---

# ❌ **When Legal Aid Usually Cannot Help**
LAO **usually does NOT help** with:
- Suing your property manager for **money only**
- Small claims court disputes (unless related to housing rights)
- Normal contract disagreements not tied to tenancy rights

For those matters, Legal Aid may refer you to:
- **Community legal clinics**
- **Tenant Duty Counsel**
- **Free housing law clinics**
