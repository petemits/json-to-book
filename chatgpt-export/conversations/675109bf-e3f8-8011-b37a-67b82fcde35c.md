# Canadian Digital Directories
*2024-12-05*

**User:**

Where can find  Canadian downloadable digital directories

**Assistant:**

To find Canadian downloadable digital directories, consider the following sources:

### **1. Canadian Business Directories**
- **Scott’s Directories**: Offers downloadable business directories for Canadian companies across industries. [Visit Scott’s Directories](https://www.scottsdirectories.com).
- **InfoCanada**: Provides access to detailed Canadian business information. You can export data in various formats. [Visit InfoCanada](https://www.infocanada.ca).

### **2. Public and Government Databases**
- **Canada Business Network**: Lists government and business directories. Some are downloadable. [Visit Canada Business Network](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/business.html).
- **Statistics Canada**: Provides open data directories and digital information. [Visit Statistics Canada](https://www.statcan.gc.ca).

### **3. Industry-Specific Directories**
- **Chamber of Commerce Listings**: Many Canadian Chambers of Commerce offer local business directories for download.
- **Yellow Pages Canada**: Some areas allow downloadable versions of their directories. [Visit Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.ca).

### **4. Online Marketplaces**
- **Etsy** and **Amazon**: Sometimes, digital versions of Canadian business directories are sold here.

### **5. Libraries and Universities**
- Check with **Library and Archives Canada** or local public libraries, which often provide access to free or paid directories for download.

If you're looking for a specific type of directory (e.g., businesses, professionals, nonprofits), let me know, and I can refine the suggestions.
