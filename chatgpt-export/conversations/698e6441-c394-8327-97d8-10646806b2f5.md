Here are the two main situations:

---

### ✅ 1. Viewing Through the Manufacturer’s App (Cloud-Based Cameras)
Most modern mini WiFi cameras (like ones from brands such as entity["company","TP-Link","networking company"], entity["company","EZVIZ","smart camera brand"], or entity["company","Wyze","smart home company"]) work like this:

- The camera streams video to the company’s cloud server.
- You log into the app from anywhere.
- As long as **both the camera and your phone have internet access**, you can view live video.

👉 In this case, you do **NOT** need to be on the same WiFi network.

---

### ⚙️ 2. Direct / Local Network Cameras (No Cloud)
Some cheaper mini WiFi cameras or IP cameras:

- Only work on the same local WiFi network.
- Do not support remote viewing unless you set up port forwarding or a VPN.
- May require technical setup.

👉 In this case, you **must be on the same network** unless you configure remote access manually.

---

### 🔎 Quick Rule
- If the camera uses a mobile app with login → works anywhere with internet.
- If it only shows up via IP address (like 192.168.x.x) → same network only.
