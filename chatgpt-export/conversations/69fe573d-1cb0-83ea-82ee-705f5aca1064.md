**Assistant:**

A valid Ontario health card or driver’s licence helps prove your identity, but by itself it is **not enough** to get a Canadian passport. You also need proof of Canadian citizenship.

For a regular adult passport application, Canada usually requires:
- proof of citizenship (citizenship certificate/card or Canadian birth certificate), and
- valid ID (driver’s licence, health card if accepted, etc.).

Since you lost the citizenship certificate and applied for a replacement, the passport office may still be able to help in urgent cases by verifying your citizenship records internally — especially if:
- you previously had a Canadian passport,
- you know your old passport number,
- or you have copies/photos of your old citizenship card.

Your Ontario driver’s licence is stronger ID for passport purposes than the health card. Ontario health cards are often restricted as primary ID because of provincial privacy rules.

You should bring:
- valid driver’s licence,
- health card (optional supporting ID),
- proof you applied for replacement citizenship certificate,
- any old passport or passport number,
- travel proof if urgent.

Official passport requirements are here:
urlCanadian Passport Adult Application Requirementshttps://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-passports/new-adult-passport.html

**Assistant:**

To replace a lost Canadian citizenship certificate/card, you apply for a “Proof of Citizenship” replacement through urlImmigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC)https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-citizenship/proof-canadian-citizenship.html.

You normally need:

- A completed application form (CIT 0001)
- Two pieces of personal ID
- Passport-style photos (if requested under current rules)
- Details about your lost certificate/card
- Processing fee payment receipt
- Supporting documents proving identity and citizenship history

Common IDs accepted include:
- Ontario driver’s licence
- Ontario photo card
- Passport
- Health card (sometimes as secondary ID)

If you became a citizen through naturalization, IRCC can usually locate your citizenship record using:
- your full legal name,
- date of birth,
- place of birth,
- previous certificate number (if known),
- immigration records,
- old passport information.

If you do not remember the citizenship certificate number, you can still apply.

You can apply online or by mail:
urlApply for a Citizenship Certificate (Proof of Citizenship)https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-citizenship/proof-canadian-citizenship/apply.html

If your travel is urgent, after submitting the replacement application you can contact passport services and ask whether they can verify citizenship directly from IRCC records while your replacement certificate is processing.

**Assistant:**

For a replacement Canadian citizenship certificate, IRCC may ask for information about your parents — such as their names, dates of birth, or places of birth — but that is usually for identity verification and record matching, not because you automatically need your parents’ documents.

What is typically required depends on your situation:

- If you were born in Canada:
  - usually a Canadian birth certificate is enough proof of citizenship.
- If you became a citizen through immigration/naturalization:
  - IRCC mainly uses your own records and IDs.
- If records are incomplete, older, damaged, or difficult to verify:
  - they may request additional supporting information, including parental details.

For a lost or stolen citizenship certificate, you normally need:
- your own ID,
- application form,
- replacement fee,
- explanation of loss/theft,
- and any old citizenship/passport details you still have.

You do not usually need your parents to attend or provide full credentials unless IRCC specifically requests extra verification.

**Assistant:**

A replacement Canadian citizenship certificate (“Proof of Citizenship”) can take several months under normal processing.

Current official processing times are posted here:
urlIRCC Citizenship Certificate Processing Timeshttps://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/check-processing-times.html

Processing times change often, but replacement certificates are commonly in the range of:
- several months for standard processing,
- sometimes faster if urgent processing is approved.

Urgent processing may be possible if you need:
- a passport urgently,
- travel for illness/death,
- employment requirements,
- government benefits,
- or other time-sensitive reasons.

You can request urgent processing with proof:
- flight tickets,
- medical letters,
- employer letters,
- travel itinerary, etc.

Urgent request info:
urlUrgent Proof of Citizenship Requestshttps://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-citizenship/proof-canadian-citizenship/urgent.html

Since you also need a passport, you may want to:
1. Ask IRCC for urgent citizenship certificate processing.
2. Bring proof of that application to a Passport Canada office.
3. Ask whether passport services can verify your citizenship record internally while the replacement is pending.
