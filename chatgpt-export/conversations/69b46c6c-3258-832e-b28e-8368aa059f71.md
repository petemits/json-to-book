# 2. Main Requirements (Attestation / Support)
To prove the party is real, you must show **public support**.

Typical requirement:

- **About 250 declarations of support** (federal level) from eligible voters.

- **Declarations of Support**
- **Petition / Attestation signatures**

You must appoint:

- **Chief Agent (federal)** or  
- **Chief Financial Officer (provincial)**

This person manages party finances.

### 4️⃣ Auditor Appointment

### 5️⃣ Supporter Declaration Forms
The **attestation signatures** from voters supporting the party.
